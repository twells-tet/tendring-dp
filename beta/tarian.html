TARIAN

TARIAN provides strategic, data-led support for candidates and campaign teams who want to win in 2027 and beyond.

We help campaigns turn electoral data into practical decisions — from voter contact data and marked register analysis to demographic insight, targeting and campaign planning.

TARIAN also provides guidance on social media and digital campaigning, helping teams understand where to focus their resources, who they need to reach and how their online activity can support the wider campaign.

Our focus is straightforward: turbocharge your fundraising, better data, sharper targeting and more effective campaigns.

We can also help you win your selection campaign, if you want to be a candidate for Parliament next time!

Previous clients include the Conservative MPs for Beverley and Holderness, Broadland and Fakenham, Havant, Keighley and Stockton West.
