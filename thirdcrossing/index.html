Resilience

When a crossing closes or traffic is restricted, everyday journeys, public services and businesses are disrupted. A third route would give the area a stronger, more dependable network.

Opportunity

A well-planned crossing can support homes, jobs, education and healthcare on both sides of the Straits, helping Anglesey and Bangor thrive together.

A Better Plan

The campaign calls for an open, evidence-led discussion that puts local communities, safety, the environment and long-term transport needs first.
